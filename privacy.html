Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

The short version

  • LogMyDrive has no accounts, no login, and no servers of its own.
  • Everything you enter — drivers, drives, routes, skills, notes — is stored only on your phone.
  • Location is used only while you're actively tracking a drive, and it never leaves your device unless you personally export a report or backup and choose where to send it.
  • We don't run ads, don't use analytics or tracking SDKs, and don't sell data — there's nothing to sell.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything, permanently.

LogMyDrive ("LogMyDrive," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile app that helps families track supervised practice-driving hours for a learner's permit. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and — just as importantly — what it deliberately does not collect. LogMyDrive was built to be usable by a family without ever requiring them to trust us with their data, and this policy is written to reflect that design, not to justify around it.

1. Information we collect

LogMyDrive does not require an account, so we do not collect your name, email address, or any identity information to use the app. The information below is entered by you and stored locally in the app's database on your own device — it is not transmitted to us or to any third party as part of normal use.

  • Driver profiles — a name, state, permit dates, and guardian name you enter for each driver.
  • Optional driver photo — if you choose to add one, taken with the camera or picked from your photo library.
  • Drive records — the date, time, duration, distance, and GPS route of each logged drive, along with any category tags, supervisor name, or notes you add.
  • Skill checklist entries — progress notes you record for driving skills like parking or highway merging.
  • App settings — preferences like light/dark mode, backup frequency, and your Pro purchase status.

2. Information we don't collect

To be direct about it, LogMyDrive does not collect, and has no mechanism to collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials — there is no account.
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics events, crash telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • Contacts, browsing history, or data from other apps.
  • Any data at all from a server, because LogMyDrive doesn't operate one. There is nothing for us to be breached, subpoenaed for, or accidentally leave public — the data genuinely never reaches us.

3. Location data, specifically

Because this is the one permission families are usually most cautious about, here is exactly how it works:

  • LogMyDrive requests location access so it can record the route, distance, and timing of a supervised drive, and so it can automatically detect whether a drive happened at night (using sunset/sunrise times for your location — calculated on your device, not looked up online).
  • Location is recorded only while a drive is actively being tracked — from the moment you tap Start Drive to the moment it ends.
  • If you enable the optional auto-stop setting, the app may continue checking your location in the background for a short time after you've stopped moving, solely to detect that the drive has ended and prompt you (or save it automatically) — this is still tied to an active, in-progress drive, never a general background tracking mode.
  • Recorded routes are stored in the app's local database like any other drive detail. They are not transmitted anywhere automatically, are not used for advertising, and are not linked to any identity we hold — because we don't hold one.

4. Camera & photo library

If you choose to add a profile photo for a driver, LogMyDrive requests camera or photo library access at that moment. The photo is copied into the app's local storage and used only to display that driver's profile picture in the app. It is not uploaded, analyzed, or shared.

5. In-app purchases

LogMyDrive offers a one-time "Pro" purchase to unlock additional features. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (or Google Play, on Android) through their standard in-app purchase systems. We never see or store your payment information — no card number, billing address, or similar details pass through LogMyDrive at any point. The app only receives confirmation of whether the purchase was completed, which it stores locally to know the feature is unlocked.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, because we do not have access to it in the first place. The only ways data ever leaves your device are actions you initiate:

  • Exporting a PDF report or a state-formatted log sheet — you choose where it goes (save it, print it, share it via Messages, email, AirDrop, etc.) using your device's standard share sheet.
  • Exporting a backup — a full backup of your data as a file you save wherever you choose (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files, email, etc.). This, too, uses your device's own share sheet; LogMyDrive does not have its own storage to send it to.
Automatic local backup snapshots, if enabled, stay on your device (and are included in your phone's own device backup, e.g. iCloud). They are not uploaded by LogMyDrive itself.

7. Storage & security

All app data is stored in a local database on your device, protected by your device's own security (such as its passcode and encryption). Because there is no server component, there is no central database of LogMyDrive users or their driving data to secure, and no cloud infrastructure that could be compromised on our end.

8. Retention & deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you delete it within the app. Uninstalling LogMyDrive permanently deletes all of its data, including drivers, drives, and any local backup snapshots — this is standard behavior for how apps store data on iOS and Android, and it means there is nothing left over on your device or anywhere else after you remove the app. If you want to keep a copy first, use the app's Export feature before uninstalling.

9. Children's privacy

LogMyDrive is intended to be used by a parent or guardian together with a teen learner driver. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone, full stop — nothing entered into the app is transmitted off the device. If you have questions about how the app fits into your family's use with a minor, contact us using the details below.

10. Your choices

  • Location: you can decline or later revoke location permission in your device settings. Without it, GPS drive tracking won't work, but you can still log drives manually.
  • Camera/Photos: entirely optional; skip it and driver profiles simply show initials instead of a photo.
  • Notifications: you can disable reminder notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Deleting your data: delete a driver profile within the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything at once.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Since the app has no way to reach you directly (again — no accounts, no push notifications tied to identity), we'd encourage checking back here occasionally if you'd like to stay current, though our intent is for this policy to stay simple because the app's actual behavior stays simple.
